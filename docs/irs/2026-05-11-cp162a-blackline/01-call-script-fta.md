## Opening (lead with FTA only — do NOT bundle 84-35)

> "Hi, I'm calling about a CP162A notice we received for our partnership — failure to file Form 1065, \$510 penalty. I'd like to request **First-Time Abate**. This was the partnership's first tax year — the entity was incorporated in June 2025, so there are no prior years for it to have any compliance history."

### Authentication

The CSR will ask you to verify the account. Give: - EIN: **39-2703617** - Entity name: **BlackLine MTB Apparel LLC** *(exactly — say "Apparel" — it's part of the legal name)* - Address on file: **24571 SE 43rd Pl, Sammamish, WA 98029-7542** - Your name and title: **Travis Sparks, Partner**

**If address authentication fails:** say "the address may have updated on a recent filing — can you authenticate via EIN, entity name, and the notice number instead?"

**If they ask for Form 2848 (power of attorney):** "I'm an authorized partner of the LLC speaking on the partnership's behalf for an informational penalty matter. Do you require a 2848 for this specific request, or can we proceed?" Most CSRs will proceed without one for FTA on a small balance.

## If they grant FTA on the call

Get all of these — **write them down in real time**:

1.  **CSR name and badge/ID number.**
2.  **Verbal confirmation:** "Can you confirm the \$510 penalty has been removed and the balance on the CP162A is now zero?"
3.  **Reference / case number** for the call.
4.  **When the confirmation letter will arrive** (ask explicitly — typical answer: 30 days).
5.  **What appears on the IRS Online Account / Business Tax Account** — "If I log in to the business account in a few days, should I see the abatement posted?"

Closing:

> "Thank you. To confirm: First-Time Abate granted, \$510 penalty removed, reference number \[X\], no payment required, confirmation letter within 30 days. Anything else I need to do on my end?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## If they decline FTA (very unlikely for a first-year entity)

If the CSR says no — politely ask for the specific reason and write it down verbatim. Then pivot:

> "Understood. Can we then process this under **reasonable cause** per IRC §6698(a)(2)? The partnership had a net loss in 2025, both partners timely filed their personal 1040s reporting their distributive share, there is no tax gap, and this was the entity's first tax year."

If reasonable cause is also declined verbally:

> "Then please note on the account that I'm submitting a written abatement request with all the supporting evidence. Can you confirm the correct mailing address for an abatement letter — is it still the Ogden address on the notice, or is there a specific stop number?"

Do NOT escalate to a supervisor on the first call unless the CSR is hostile — supervisors are bottlenecks. Mail the letter and let the written request route through a penalty abatement coordinator.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## If they say "I can't process this on the phone — send it in writing"

This is a common deflection. Don't argue. Respond:

> "Understood. Can you note on the account that an abatement request is pending in writing, and give me a reference number for this call so the written request ties back?"

Then mail the letter the same day.

## What NOT to say

-   **Don't bundle FTA and Rev. Proc. 84-35 in the opening.** FTA is a mechanical transcript check; the CSR can grant it without thinking. Bundling 84-35 invites them to deny the package.
-   **Don't volunteer the Emerson-is-a-partner-but-only-0%-vested detail unprompted.** It's true and defensible, but it opens a tangent on profits-interest treatment that doesn't help your \$510 case. If asked who the partners are, say "Travis Sparks and Emerson Sparks." That's it.
-   **Don't agree to a payment plan or commit to paying.** If offered, say "I'd like to resolve the abatement first before discussing payment."
-   **Don't describe extenuating circumstances** unless FTA and reasonable cause are both off the table.
-   **Don't say "automatic abatement"** — say "first-time abate" or "abatement under IRM 20.1.1.3.6.1.1."

## If the rep asks why the 1065 was filed a month late

Honest, brief answer: "This was the partnership's first tax year — entity incorporated in June 2025. We filed by 4/15 along with our personal 1040s. We've now calendared 3/15 for next year." Do not over-explain. The reason is not relevant to FTA; it only matters for reasonable cause.
