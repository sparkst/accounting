        is now calendared and will be observed.

The Service has long applied IRC §6698(a)(2) reasonable-cause

revenue loss. The partnership respectfully submits that this
standard is met.

## Notes on the draft

- **Three independent grounds** (FTA + reasonable cause + 84-35). Examiners who reject one path on policy grounds (e.g., applying the post-BBA position against 84-35) still have two remaining grounds to grant. If any single one is granted, the full $510 comes off.
- **Form 843 is the cover** — addresses the procedural-form concern. Some Ogden compliance units set aside free-form letters with a request to refile on Form 843; attaching it pre-empts that delay.
- **No payment enclosed.** Per IRC §6601, interest on $510 over 60–90 days is approximately $10–15 — a bounded, trivial cost relative to the chase-the-refund risk if payment is sent and abatement is granted.
- **Appeal-rights preservation clause** is included so a denial doesn't quietly foreclose Appeals.
- **Declaration under penalty of perjury** is qualified with "to the best of my knowledge and belief after reasonable inquiry" — narrows IRC §7206 exposure without weakening the substantive claim.
- **Entity name uses "BlackLine MTB Apparel LLC"** — the full legal name on the TaxAct e-file and the CP162A notice.
